*** FAIRLIGHT TRIBUTE FROM ARGENTINA ***

RICO OF PVM TAKES ADVANTAGE OF FLASHPARTY'S GLOBAL REACH TO PRESENT THIS HUMBLE TRIBUTE TO FAIRLIGHT'S LEGENDARY INTRO.

SEEING THE ICONIC LOGO ON SCREEN AND HEARING THE OPENING NOTES OF THE DRUID II SIDCHIP MUSIC CARRIES A DEEP EMOTIONAL WEIGHT.

NOW, LET ME TELL YOU ABOUT THE EBCM CHARACTER LOGO I CREATED FOR THIS INTRO, AND WHY I CHOSE BLUE AND GOLD FOR FAIRLIGHT'S LOGO. BEHIND THOSE COLORS LIES A REMARKABLE TWIST OF FATE, BRIDGING SWEDEN AND ARGENTINA.

IN 1907, AS LEGEND HAS IT, BOCA JUNIORS CHOSE ITS COLORS FROM THE FLAG OF THE NEXT SHIP TO DOCK IN BUENOS AIRES: THE SWEDISH DROTTNING SOPHIA.

SWEDEN GAVE BOCA ITS BLUE AND GOLD; ARGENTINA MADE THEM IMMORTAL IN THE SHIRT OF D10S HIMSELF.

NOW, MORE THAN A CENTURY LATER, THOSE VERY COLORS RETURN TO SWEDEN ON THE LOGO OF FAIRLIGHT, THE GODS WHO MADE OUR COMMODORE 64 DREAMS COME ALIVE.

A CENTURY-OLD VOYAGE COMES FULL CIRCLE: FROM A SWEDISH FLAG TO AN ARGENTINE PASSION, AND BACK AS A HUMBLE OFFERING OF LOVE AND MEMORY.
